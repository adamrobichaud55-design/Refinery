# Credits for the sample tour

The tour and the gallery pictures in this demo come from the **"nyc" scene of the Zip-NeRF dataset** (Jonathan T. Barron,
Ben Mildenhall, Dor Verbin, Pratul P. Srinivasan, Peter Hedman, "Zip-NeRF: Anti-Aliased Grid-Based Neural Radiance
Fields", 2023, Google Research): 990 photographs of one apartment, with the position each was taken from.

They were downloaded from the `nvs-bench/zipnerf` repository on Hugging Face, which is marked with the MIT licence.
The dataset was made for research. Before using these pictures in anything you sell, check the dataset's own terms;
for client work this does not arise, because the tour is built from the client's own video or photographs.

The apartment is not for sale. The address, price and listing details on the demo page are made up.

The frames in `walk/` are resized copies of those photographs, each with a depth map made from it by the
Depth Anything V2 model (Apache-2.0). The room names were found by the CLIP model (MIT).
