Generated by `tools/refinery-process.ts` from `src/refinery/`; do not edit by hand. Rules: DESIGN_BASIS.md §74 (`SW-REF-*`) and §75 (`SW-PRC-*`).

**What this is.** The process the model is built on: what the refinery makes from its crude, what each unit does, every equipment item with the size its duty calls for, and every stream that passes between units. The 3D model's equipment and piping are sized and connected from this and nothing else.

**What it is not.** It is an invented demonstration, not a process design. The crude, the yields, the operating conditions and the sizing rules are round figures chosen by SmartWeld from general refining knowledge, with the reasoning written in the design basis; no handbook, licensor's design, assay or published flow scheme was opened or reproduced. Every figure is for the owner to review and tune.

**Size.** 16 units, 374 tagged equipment items (511 machines, shells and bays with installed spares and parallel shells), 615 process lines and 127 utility connections to equipment, before battery-limit stations, relief lines, vents, drains and hose stations are added by the piping stage.

**Hydrogen** (SW-REF-7). The reformer makes 2,146 lb/h (9.69 million standard cubic feet a day). The naphtha and kerosene hydrotreaters take 136 and 183 lb/h in net gas; 1,827 lb/h goes to the adsorbers, which recover 1,589 lb/h; the diesel hydrotreater takes 997 lb/h, leaving a surplus of 592 lb/h (2.67 million standard cubic feet a day) for fuel gas. The refinery lives on its own hydrogen.

**Sulfur** (SW-REF-7). The rest of the crude's sulfur leaves in fuel oil, asphalt and the cracker's liquid products.

- **11 Crude distillation.** Separates the crude by boiling range into gas, naphtha, kerosene, diesel, gas oil and residue, after washing the salt out of it. Everything else in the refinery works on what this unit cuts.
- **12 Vacuum distillation.** Distils the atmospheric residue again under vacuum, so that gas oil for the cracker can be boiled off without the oil being hot enough to crack. What will not boil leaves as vacuum residue for fuel oil and asphalt.
- **13 Naphtha hydrotreater and splitter.** Removes sulfur and nitrogen from the naphtha with hydrogen over a catalyst, because they would poison the reformer's catalyst, then splits it into light naphtha for gasoline blending and heavy naphtha for the reformer.
- **14 Catalytic reformer and hydrogen purification.** Rearranges heavy naphtha into high-octane reformate for gasoline, and in doing so makes the hydrogen the hydrotreaters live on. Its adsorbers purify that hydrogen for the diesel hydrotreater.
- **15 Kerosene hydrotreater.** Removes sulfur from kerosene with hydrogen so that it meets jet fuel quality.
- **16 Diesel hydrotreater.** Removes sulfur from diesel and light cycle oil with hydrogen at high pressure to make road diesel.
- **17 Fluid catalytic cracker and gas concentration.** Cracks gas oil on hot fluidised catalyst into gasoline and liquefied gas, burning the coke off the catalyst to supply the heat. Its gas plant recovers and treats the refinery's fuel gas, liquefied gas and cracked gasoline.
- **18 Sulfur block.** Takes the hydrogen sulfide out of circulation: regenerates the amine that washes it from the gases, strips it from sour water, and converts it to liquid sulfur for sale.
- **19 Utilities.** Supplies what every unit needs to run: steam, boiler feed water, cooling water, compressed air, nitrogen, caustic and fuel gas.
- **20 Crude tank farm.** Receives crude from tankers and holds two weeks of it, so that the crude unit runs steadily between cargoes.
- **21 Intermediate and product tank farm.** Holds feed between units, blends gasoline from its components, and stores the finished products and liquefied gas until they are shipped.
- **22 Marine terminal.** Lands crude from tankers and loads products onto ships at a berth in deep water.
- **23 Truck loading.** Loads gasoline, jet fuel, diesel, asphalt and liquefied gas onto road tankers.
- **24 Wastewater treatment.** Cleans the refinery's waste water (desalter brine, stripped water, blowdown and oily drainage) before it goes to the outfall, and recovers the oil.
- **25 Flare.** Burns safely, at a distance, whatever the relief valves release in an upset.
- **26 Fire water.** Holds and pumps the water that fights a fire anywhere on the site.

Unit 27 is the buildings, substations and site works, which carry no process.
