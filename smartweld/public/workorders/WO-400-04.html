Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 3"-P300-P-1015.
  2. Break the joint at E 68'-6" N 17'-8-13/16" EL 125'-0" between VALVE, CONTROL, GLOBE WITH DIAPHRAGM ACTUATOR 3" P300 and VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 3" P300.

  4. Fit a new gasket and new studs; tighten to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  5. Remove blinds; leak test on start-up (SW-TEST-2).
