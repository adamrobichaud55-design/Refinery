WO-200-03 — Replace VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 4" P300 (4"-P300-P-1021/A2)

AreaA200 Vessels and columnTaskVR Valve replacement in kind
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetV-102 — V-102 vertical vessel 72" ID × 216" T/T — E 53'-6" N -25'-0" EL 100'-8-1/4"
EquipmentV-102Lines4"-P300-P-1021
IsometricsISO-4"-P300-P-1021 (generated); owner's drawings ISO-P-1021
PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Outlet isolation valve stem packing leaking and seat passing.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 4"-P300-P-1021 between the isolations.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 4" P300 (4"-P300-P-1021/A2) at E 53'-6" N -20'-8-3/16" EL 109'-0".
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5); dress or report damage.
  4. Install a new VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 4" P300 of the same size, class and face-to-face (GATE4-P300), with new gaskets and studs, stem orientation as removed.
  5. Tighten the studs to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, leak test the two joints on start-up (SW-TEST-2).

Affected items: 4"-P300-P-1021/A2, 4"-P300-P-1021/A1, 4"-P300-P-1021/A4

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
