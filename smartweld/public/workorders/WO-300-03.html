PermitsGeneral work permit, Confined-space entry permit

Reason

Scheduled internal inspection of the drum.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate D-101 per the isolation list; drain, vent, purge and gas-test for entry.
  2. Open for entry; clean as needed for inspection.
  3. Internal visual examination; ultrasonic thickness at the 14 TMLs.
  4. Close with new gaskets on every opened joint; remove blinds; restore the line-up.

Affected items: D-101

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-6
