WO-500-03 — Replace spool SP03 of 1"-P300-HS-5013

AreaA500 Pipe rackTaskSR Spool replacement
Priority3 (reliability)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetrack-bent:720 — Pipe rack bent at E 60'-0" — E 60'-0" N 0.000" EL 122'-0"
Equipment—Lines1"-P300-HS-5013
IsometricsISO-1"-P300-HS-5013 (generated); owner's drawings ISO-HS-5013
PermitsGeneral work permit, Hot work permit

Reason

Steam hose-station drop corroded at the elbow.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list; drain, vent and purge 1"-P300-HS-5013 between the isolations; confirm gas-free for hot work.
  2. Remove spool 1"-P300-HS-5013-SP03 (5 items, 33'-1-3/16" of pipe) — cut at the field welds and break the flanged joints at its ends as listed.
  3. Fabricate the replacement spool to ISO-1"-P300-HS-5013 with the same items (parts list), welding to WS-01; 4 shop welds.
  4. Install the spool; make 2 field welds; new gaskets and studs at flanged ends, tightened per SW-WO-5.
  5. NDE per the NDE table; hydrostatic test at 420 psi for 30 minutes with the relief valves blinded (SW-TEST-1).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, hand back.

Affected items: 1"-P300-HS-5013/p8, 1"-P300-HS-5013/el9, 1"-P300-HS-5013/p10, 1"-P300-HS-5013/el11, 1"-P300-HS-5013/p12
