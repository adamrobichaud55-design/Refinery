Reason

Thickness survey of the steam header.

Scope of work

  1. Erect access and remove insulation at the TMLs where fitted (class S300, insulated).
  2. Calibrate the thickness gauge on a step block covering 0.21875 in.
  3. Take four readings 90° apart at each of the 23 TMLs (SW-WO-6).
  4. Report readings against the owner's minimum wall; raise a spool replacement for any reading below it.
