Reason

Scheduled internal inspection of the vessel.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate V-101 per the isolation list; drain, vent, purge and gas-test for entry.
  2. Open for entry; clean as needed for inspection.
  3. Internal visual examination; ultrasonic thickness at the 14 TMLs.
  4. Close with new gaskets on every opened joint; remove blinds; restore the line-up.

Affected items: V-101
