WO-400-05 — Replace spool SP04 of 4"-P300-P-1019

AreaA400 PumpsTaskSR Spool replacement
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Equipment—Lines4"-P300-P-1019, 3/4"-P300-I-7019, 3/4"-P300-SP-7028
IsometricsISO-4"-P300-P-1019, ISO-3/4"-P300-I-7019, ISO-3/4"-P300-SP-7028
PermitsGeneral work permit, Hot work permit

Reason

Erosion at the pump discharge bend.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list; drain, vent and purge 4"-P300-P-1019 between the isolations; confirm gas-free for hot work.
  2. Remove spool 4"-P300-P-1019-SP04 (12 items, 17'-8-13/16" of pipe) — cut at the field welds and break the flanged joints at its ends as listed.
  3. Fabricate the replacement spool to ISO-4"-P300-P-1019 with the same items (parts list), welding to WS-01; 11 shop welds.
  4. Install the spool; make 4 field welds; new gaskets and studs at flanged ends, tightened per SW-WO-5.
  5. NDE per the NDE table; hydrostatic test at 420 psi for 30 minutes with the relief valves blinded (SW-TEST-1).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, hand back.

Affected items: 4"-P300-P-1019/el21, 4"-P300-P-1019/p22, 4"-P300-P-1019/el23, 4"-P300-P-1019/p24, 4"-P300-P-1019/T:3/4"-P300-I-7019, 4"-P300-P-1019/p26, 4"-P300-P-1019/T:3/4"-P300-SP-7028, 4"-P300-P-1019/p28, 4"-P300-P-1019/el29, 4"-P300-P-1019/p30, 4"-P300-P-1019/el31, 4"-P300-P-1019/p32
