Reason

Steam leak at the tank heating-coil inlet flange.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 2"-P300-LS-3007.
  2. Break the joint at E 22'-0" N -12" EL 123'-9-1/4" between FLANGE WELD-NECK 2" P300 RAISED FACE and VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 2" P300.
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5).
  4. Fit a new gasket and new studs; tighten to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  5. Remove blinds; leak test on start-up (SW-TEST-2).
