PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Control valve trim worn; replace the valve in kind.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 4"-P300-P-1005 between the isolations.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, CONTROL, GLOBE WITH DIAPHRAGM ACTUATOR 4" P300 (4"-P300-P-1005/B13) at E 33'-6" N -15'-7-15/16" EL 121'-0".
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5); dress or report damage.
  4. Install a new VALVE, CONTROL, GLOBE WITH DIAPHRAGM ACTUATOR 4" P300 of the same size, class and face-to-face (CONTROL4-P300), with new gaskets and studs, stem orientation as removed.
  5. Tighten the studs to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, leak test the two joints on start-up (SW-TEST-2).

Affected items: 4"-P300-P-1005/B13, 4"-P300-P-1005/B15, 4"-P300-P-1005/B12

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
