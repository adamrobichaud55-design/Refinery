WO-400-03 — Replace VALVE, SWING CHECK 3" P300 (4"-P300-P-1008/A2)

AreaA400 PumpsTaskVR Valve replacement in kind
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetP-102A — P-102A centrifugal pump — E 164'-0" N -18'-6" EL 100'-9"
EquipmentP-102ALines4"-P300-P-1008
IsometricsISO-4"-P300-P-1008 (generated); owner's drawings ISO-P-1008
PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Discharge check valve slamming; replace in kind.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 4"-P300-P-1008 between the isolations.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, SWING CHECK 3" P300 (4"-P300-P-1008/A2) at E 164'-0" N -16'-6" EL 104'-1/8".
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5); dress or report damage.
  4. Install a new VALVE, SWING CHECK 3" P300 of the same size, class and face-to-face (CHECK3-P300), with new gaskets and studs, stem orientation as removed.
  5. Tighten the studs to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, leak test the two joints on start-up (SW-TEST-2).

Affected items: 4"-P300-P-1008/A2, 4"-P300-P-1008/A1, 4"-P300-P-1008/A4

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
