WO-200-04 — Ultrasonic thickness survey of 6"-P300-P-1011

PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Thickness survey of the column overhead line.

Scope of work

  1. Erect access and remove insulation at the TMLs where fitted (class H300).
  2. Calibrate the thickness gauge on a step block covering 0.21875 in.
  3. Take four readings 90° apart at each of the 15 TMLs (SW-WO-6).
  4. Report readings against the owner's minimum wall; raise a spool replacement for any reading below it.
