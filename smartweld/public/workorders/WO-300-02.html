WO-300-02 — Pull, clean and test the tube bundle of E-103

AreaA300 Exchangers and drumTaskXB Exchanger bundle pull, clean and tube-side test
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
EquipmentE-103Lines4"-P150-CWS-3005, 4"-P150-CWR-3006, 6"-P300-P-1011, 6"-P300-P-1012, 1"-P150-V-6027, 1"-P150-D-6028, 3/4"-P300-I-6029
IsometricsISO-4"-P150-CWS-3005, ISO-4"-P150-CWR-3006, ISO-6"-P300-P-1011, ISO-6"-P300-P-1012, ISO-1"-P150-V-6027, ISO-1"-P150-D-6028, ISO-3/4"-P300-I-6029
PermitsGeneral work permit, Confined-space entry permit, Lifting plan

Reason

Fouling: tube bundle to be pulled, cleaned and tested.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate E-103 per the isolation list (tube and shell sides); drain and vent.
  2. Remove the channel cover; pull the tube bundle to the cleaning bay.
  3. Clean the tubes and shell side; inspect visually; thickness at the TMLs.
  4. Reinstall the bundle with new gaskets; tube-side hydrostatic test at 210 psi (SW-TEST-1).
  5. Remove blinds; restore the line-up.

Affected items: E-103

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-TEST-1, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-6
