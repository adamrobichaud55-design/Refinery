WO-200-05 — Replace spool SP02 of 6"-P300-P-1017

AreaA200 Vessels and columnTaskSR Spool replacement
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetC-101 — C-101 vertical vessel 48" ID × 240" T/T — E 68'-6" N 25'-0" EL 100'-8-1/4"
EquipmentC-101Lines6"-P300-P-1017
IsometricsISO-P-1017 (owner's drawings, attached)
PermitsGeneral work permit, Hot work permit

Reason

Wall loss below the owner's minimum found by the last survey on the column bottoms line.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list; drain, vent and purge 6"-P300-P-1017 between the isolations; confirm gas-free for hot work.
  2. Remove spool 6"-P300-P-1017-SP02 (8 items, 23'-5-1/2" of pipe) — cut at the field welds and break the flanged joints at its ends as listed.
  3. Fabricate the replacement spool to ISO-6"-P300-P-1017 with the same items (parts list), welding to WS-01; 7 shop welds.
  4. Install the spool; make 2 field welds; new gaskets and studs at flanged ends, tightened per SW-WO-5.
  5. NDE per the NDE table; hydrostatic test at 420 psi for 30 minutes with the relief valves blinded (SW-TEST-1).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, hand back.

Affected items: 6"-P300-P-1017/p7, 6"-P300-P-1017/el8, 6"-P300-P-1017/p9, 6"-P300-P-1017/el10, 6"-P300-P-1017/p11, 6"-P300-P-1017/el12, 6"-P300-P-1017/p13, 6"-P300-P-1017/el14
