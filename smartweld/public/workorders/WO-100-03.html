WO-100-03 — Replace spool SP02 of 6"-P150-P-1001

AreaA100 Tank farmTaskSR Spool replacement
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetT-101 — T-101 tank 12' dia × 22' high — E 25'-0" N 26'-6" EL 100'-6"
EquipmentT-101Lines6"-P150-P-1001
IsometricsISO-6"-P150-P-1001 (generated); owner's drawings ISO-P-1001
PermitsGeneral work permit, Hot work permit

Reason

External corrosion found on the tank outlet line at the first bend.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list; drain, vent and purge 6"-P150-P-1001 between the isolations; confirm gas-free for hot work.
  2. Remove spool 6"-P150-P-1001-SP02 (7 items, 37'-0" of pipe) — cut at the field welds and break the flanged joints at its ends as listed.
  3. Fabricate the replacement spool to ISO-6"-P150-P-1001 with the same items (parts list), welding to WS-01; 6 shop welds.
  4. Install the spool; make 2 field welds; new gaskets and studs at flanged ends, tightened per SW-WO-5.
  5. NDE per the NDE table; hydrostatic test at 210 psi for 30 minutes with the relief valves blinded (SW-TEST-1).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, hand back.

Affected items: 6"-P150-P-1001/el7, 6"-P150-P-1001/p8, 6"-P150-P-1001/el9, 6"-P150-P-1001/p10, 6"-P150-P-1001/el11, 6"-P150-P-1001/p12, 6"-P150-P-1001/el13
