Reason

Relief valve due for its periodic bench test.

Scope of work

  1. Confirm D-101 depressured and isolated.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, PRESSURE SAFETY VALVE 3" P300 INLET × 4" P150 OUTLET (3"-P300-RV-4001/2); blind the nozzle and the outlet line.
  3. Bench test: set pressure 300 psi and seat tightness (SW-TEST-3); service the valve as found.
  4. Reinstall with new gaskets and studs, tightened per SW-WO-5; remove blinds.

Affected items: 3"-P300-RV-4001/2, 3"-P300-RV-4001/1, 4"-P150-FL-4004/A1
