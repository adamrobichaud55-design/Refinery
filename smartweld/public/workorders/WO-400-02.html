WO-400-02 — Overhaul pump P-103B (mechanical seal and bearings)

AreaA400 PumpsTaskPO Pump overhaul
Priority3 (reliability)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetP-103B — P-103B centrifugal pump — E 179'-0" N -18'-6" EL 100'-9"
EquipmentP-103BLines4"-P300-P-1014, 3"-P300-P-1016, 3/4"-P150-W-3022
IsometricsISO-P-1014, ISO-P-1016, ISO-W-3022 (owner's drawings, attached)
PermitsGeneral work permit, Lifting plan

Reason

High bearing vibration; overhaul seal and bearings.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate P-103B electrically and per the isolation list; drain the casing.
  2. Record as-found shaft alignment; remove the coupling guard and spacer.
  3. Break the suction and discharge nozzle joints; remove the pump to the workshop.
  4. Replace the mechanical seal and the bearings; inspect the impeller and wear rings.
  5. Reinstall; new nozzle gaskets and studs tightened per SW-WO-5; align to the owner's alignment criteria.
  6. Remove blinds and isolations; run test.

Affected items: P-103B
