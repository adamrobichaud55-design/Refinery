WO-500-04 — Replace VALVE, BALL, FLOATING 1" P150 (1"-P150-HS-5011/A3)

AreaA500 Pipe rackTaskVR Valve replacement in kind
Priority3 (reliability)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)

Equipment—Lines1"-P150-HS-5011
IsometricsISO-HS-5011 (owner's drawings, attached)
PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Hose-station take-off valve at the water header seized.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 1"-P150-HS-5011 between the isolations.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, BALL, FLOATING 1" P150 (1"-P150-HS-5011/A3) at E 62'-6" N -4'-0" EL 123'-7-1/2".
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5); dress or report damage.
  4. Install a new VALVE, BALL, FLOATING 1" P150 of the same size, class and face-to-face (BALL1-P150), with new gaskets and studs, stem orientation as removed.
  5. Tighten the studs to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, leak test the two joints on start-up (SW-TEST-2).

Affected items: 1"-P150-HS-5011/A3, 1"-P150-HS-5011/A2, 1"-P150-HS-5011/A5

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
