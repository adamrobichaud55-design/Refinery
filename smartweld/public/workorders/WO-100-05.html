WO-100-05 — Replace VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 2" P150 (2"-P150-SC-3010/A2)

PermitsGeneral work permit

Reason

Condensate isolation valve passing; replace in kind.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list, drain and vent 2"-P150-SC-3010 between the isolations.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 2" P150 (2"-P150-SC-3010/A2) at E 37'-5/8" N 26'-6" EL 104'-6".
  3. Inspect both flange faces (SW-NDE-5); dress or report damage.
  4. Install a new VALVE, GATE, WEDGE, OS&Y 2" P150 of the same size, class and face-to-face (GATE2-P150), with new gaskets and studs, stem orientation as removed.
  5. Tighten the studs to the stated preload in three passes, cross pattern (SW-WO-5).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, leak test the two joints on start-up (SW-TEST-2).

Affected items: 2"-P150-SC-3010/A2, 2"-P150-SC-3010/A1, 2"-P150-SC-3010/A4

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-2, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
