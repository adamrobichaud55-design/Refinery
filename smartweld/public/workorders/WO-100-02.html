PermitsGeneral work permit, Lifting plan

Reason

Relief valve due for its periodic bench test.

Scope of work

  1. Confirm T-101 depressured and isolated.
  2. Unbolt and remove VALVE, PRESSURE SAFETY VALVE 3" P150 INLET × 4" P150 OUTLET (3"-P150-RV-4002/2); blind the nozzle and the outlet line.
  3. Bench test: set pressure 150 psi and seat tightness (SW-TEST-3); service the valve as found.
  4. Reinstall with new gaskets and studs, tightened per SW-WO-5; remove blinds.

Affected items: 3"-P150-RV-4002/2, 3"-P150-RV-4002/1, 4"-P150-FL-4005/A1

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-NDE-5, SW-TEST-3, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-7
