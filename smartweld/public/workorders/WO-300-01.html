WO-300-01 — Pull, clean and test the tube bundle of E-101

AreaA300 Exchangers and drumTaskXB Exchanger bundle pull, clean and tube-side test
Priority2 (integrity)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetE-101 — E-101 shell-and-tube exchanger 36" ID × 192" — E 111'-6" N -25'-0" EL 106'-6"
EquipmentE-101Lines4"-P300-P-1003, 4"-P300-P-1005, 4"-P150-CWS-3001, 4"-P150-CWR-3002, 1"-P150-V-6021, 1"-P150-D-6022, 3/4"-P300-I-6023
IsometricsISO-P-1003, ISO-P-1005, ISO-CWS-3001, ISO-CWR-3002, ISO-V-6021, ISO-D-6022, ISO-I-6023 (owner's drawings, attached)
PermitsGeneral work permit, Confined-space entry permit, Lifting plan

Reason

Fouling: tube bundle to be pulled, cleaned and tested.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate E-101 per the isolation list (tube and shell sides); drain and vent.
  2. Remove the channel cover; pull the tube bundle to the cleaning bay.
  3. Clean the tubes and shell side; inspect visually; thickness at the TMLs.
  4. Reinstall the bundle with new gaskets; tube-side hydrostatic test at 420 psi (SW-TEST-1).
  5. Remove blinds; restore the line-up.

Affected items: E-101

Rules: SW-ITP-1, SW-ITP-2, SW-ITP-3, SW-TEST-1, SW-WO-1, SW-WO-2, SW-WO-3, SW-WO-5, SW-WO-6, SW-WO-7
