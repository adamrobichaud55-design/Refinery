WO-300-06 — Replace spool SP03 of 4"-P150-CWS-3003

AreaA300 Exchangers and drumTaskSR Spool replacement
Priority3 (reliability)Issued byFacility owner (demonstration)
Primary assetE-102 — E-102 shell-and-tube exchanger 36" ID × 192" — E 134'-6" N -25'-0" EL 106'-6"
EquipmentE-102Lines4"-P150-CWS-3003
IsometricsISO-4"-P150-CWS-3003 (generated); owner's drawings ISO-CWS-3003
PermitsGeneral work permit, Hot work permit

Reason

Internal corrosion on the cooling water supply branch.

Scope of work

  1. Isolate per the isolation list; drain, vent and purge 4"-P150-CWS-3003 between the isolations; confirm gas-free for hot work.
  2. Remove spool 4"-P150-CWS-3003-SP03 (5 items, 39'-7-5/16" of pipe) — cut at the field welds and break the flanged joints at its ends as listed.
  3. Fabricate the replacement spool to ISO-4"-P150-CWS-3003 with the same items (parts list), welding to WS-01; 4 shop welds.
  4. Install the spool; make 2 field welds; new gaskets and studs at flanged ends, tightened per SW-WO-5.
  5. NDE per the NDE table; hydrostatic test at 210 psi for 30 minutes with the relief valves blinded (SW-TEST-1).
  6. Remove blinds, restore the line-up, hand back.

Affected items: 4"-P150-CWS-3003/p8, 4"-P150-CWS-3003/el9, 4"-P150-CWS-3003/p10, 4"-P150-CWS-3003/el11, 4"-P150-CWS-3003/p12
